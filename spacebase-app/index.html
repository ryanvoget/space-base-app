How to Play

Goal

Be the first player to reach 40 Victory Points. On each turn, roll dice to earn credits and income, then spend credits to buy better ships.

Your Spaceship

Each player has 12 sectors (numbered 2–12). Every sector holds one stationed ship (active on your roll) and any number of deployed ships (passive on others' rolls).

Taking a Turn

  • Roll two dice. Choose to use the total or split them individually.
  • Active rewards: your stationed card in the matching sector fires.
  • Passive rewards (everyone else): deployed cards in matching sectors fire for all other players.
  • Buy one ship from the shop, or pass.

Buying Ships

Ships cost Credits shown on the card. When you buy, choose to:

  • Station it — replaces the active ship in that sector. The old ship is deployed (passive).
  • Deploy it — place it passively in any sector you control.

Rewards

  • Credits Spent to buy ships
  • Income Paid out in credits at the start of your turn (max 10)
  • VP Victory points — reach 40 to win

Special Abilities

Some ships have unique abilities like arrows (fire a second sector), charge cubes (spend to activate), or set-die effects. These are described on each card in italic text.

Winning

The first player whose VP reaches 40 at the end of their turn wins. If multiple players reach 40 in the same round, the one with the most VP wins.
